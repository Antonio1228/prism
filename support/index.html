How to play

The goal
A beam shoots from each emitter. Rotate the mirrors so the light reaches every target ring in its matching colour.

Mirrors
Tap a glowing mirror to flip it between / and \. Each flip bends the beam 90°.

Filters
A coloured filter tints the beam passing through it — white light through a red filter becomes red.

Par
Par is the fewest moves needed. Beat it for a ⭐️.

Frequently asked

I bought Pro on another device / reinstalled.
Open Settings → Prism Pro → Restore purchases.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Settings → Prism Pro → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel any time; Pro stays active until the period ends.

Is there a free version?
Yes. The daily puzzle, limited daily practice, and one hint a day are free. Pro unlocks unlimited play, hints, the full archive, themes and stats.

Where is my data?
Everything is stored on your device. Clear it any time in Settings → Clear local data.

I lost my streak.
Streaks continue as long as you solve each day's puzzle. Solve today's daily to start a new one.
