Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Short version: Your puzzles, progress, collection and capsule results stay on your device. Prism has no account requirement, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and does not sell personal data. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information only when needed to provide Prism Pro.

簡要說明：題目、進度、收藏與轉蛋結果都保留在你的裝置上。Prism 不要求帳號、不顯示廣告、不使用第三方分析，也不販售個人資料。只有在提供 Prism Pro 權限所需時，Apple 與 RevenueCat 才會處理購買資訊。

Information we collect

Prism does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Purchases & subscriptions

If you buy Prism Pro, the transaction is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify and manage your subscription entitlement. For this purpose a non-identifying app user ID and your purchase history are processed. This is used solely to deliver the features you paid for. It is not used to track you and is not linked to your identity. See Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies for details.

Capsules and random cosmetics

Capsules contain cosmetic board auras only and never improve puzzle performance. Tickets cannot be purchased, traded, gifted, redeemed for money, or transferred to another person. The odds are shown before every draw: Common 70%, Rare 25%, and Legendary 5%. Every capsule contains one item, duplicate protection applies within each rarity, and the tenth draw after nine Common results is guaranteed Rare or better.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, Prism schedules a local notification on your device. No push server is involved and no data is sent to us.

Children

Prism is not listed in the Kids category. Because it includes randomized cosmetic capsules, its App Store age-rating questionnaire declares Loot Boxes and the global rating is expected to be 9+. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Data deletion

You can erase all local data at any time in Settings → Clear local data. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.
